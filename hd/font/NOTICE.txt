Dragon Warrior IV HD dialog font (proof of concept)

Every HD glyph in font.png was rendered by tools/make_font_hdpack.py from
DejaVu Sans Mono Bold. DejaVu fonts are free to use and redistribute under the
Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license (https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html).

No artwork from the original game is included. hires.txt identifies each
glyph by the 16 bytes the game writes to CHR RAM (Mesen HD pack format), so
the replacement follows the font wherever the game draws it.
